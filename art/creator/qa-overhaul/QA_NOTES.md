# Brightvale character overhaul checks

The custom sprites use a curved neck that meets the collar, with class-specific shoulder edges. The female outfits have more distinct chest, waist and hip proportions, with the arms, cuffs and held equipment anchored through the reshape. The Ranger's cast release has a continuous forearm and cuff in front of the string.
